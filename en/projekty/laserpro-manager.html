Origin

It started with buying a laser

LaserPro Manager was born from passion. Buying a diode laser as a hobby quickly grew into a business — and with it came questions no available software could answer well: how much does this product cost to make? What's the margin? Where's the material stock? Where's the order history?

There was no solution on the market that combined all these elements at an accessible price. Available programs were either too expensive, too narrow, or simply didn't understand the specifics of the laser industry. I decided to build it myself — for me, and for everyone working in this space.

LaserPro Manager is a project I develop as an enthusiast and practitioner — every feature comes from real hands-on experience with diode lasers and a growing understanding of the needs of the whole industry: diode, CO2 and fiber.

The project is globally scalable — serving users from multiple countries, billing through Stripe, interface available in many languages. The security of users' business data is an absolute priority.

System modules

🧮
Calculator

An advanced tool for calculating product cost and final price. Takes into account project files, materials and their costs, laser run time, energy, preparation and margin. Precise quoting — no guessing.

📋
Price list

Products from the calculator are saved as price list items — ready to present to clients as a quote. Photos, price, discount options, print and electronic version. Orders and sales are placed directly from here.

💰
Sales

Full sales history with net, gross and VAT data. A clear profit and loss overview — no spreadsheets needed.

📦
Orders

Order management module — status, deadlines, client and product links. Full cycle from receipt to completion.

🏪
Inventory

Define materials, add-ons and suppliers. Manage waste and stock levels. Always know what you have and where to get more.

🌐
Project library

A community space where users share or sell their projects. Inspiration and ready-to-use files — directly within the app ecosystem.

Scale and reach

LaserPro Manager is designed for the global market. The program is translated into 13 languages and supports 16 currencies — exchange rates are updated automatically on every launch.

  • 13 interface languages — available to users worldwide in their own language.
  • 16 currencies with live rates — conversions based on the current rate fetched at startup.
  • Stripe — 3 licence models — monthly, quarterly and annual. Auto-renewal, secure payments.
  • Data isolation — every company's data is fully isolated via Row Level Security at the database level.

Who it's for

The system is aimed at businesses and hobbyists working with laser engraving and cutting — diode, CO2 and fiber. Anyone who wants to stop managing orders through Excel and start working professionally — without spending a fortune on complex ERP software.
